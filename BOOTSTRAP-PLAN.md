# Jaiza — Bootstrapped Student Launch Plan (₨0–30k budget)

Scope verdict: **HIGH** — AI spend is exploding; CFOs, procurement/compliance, and boards are forced
buyers; EU AI Act enforcement (Aug 2026+) increases demand for the certificate. Wedge: Pakistani exporters
serving US/EU clients who can't answer vendor questionnaires. Risk is distribution + trust, not scope.

Core principle: **sell the service first, build the product with service revenue.**

## 90-day plan

### Days 1–14 — make the site convert (all free)
1. Calendly link as primary "Book the audit" CTA (keep mailto fallback)
2. Promote the free scan + "Load sample data" (already built) — best lead magnet
3. Publish redacted sample deliverables (1-page ledger PDF + certificate PDF) — trust asset #1
4. 3 SEO articles: "how to answer AI vendor questionnaires", "AI spend audit Pakistan",
   "SOC 2 questionnaire for software exporters" → site + LinkedIn

### Days 15–45 — 10 conversations → 2–3 paid audits
- Target: Pakistani software houses with US/EU clients (LinkedIn, PSEB directory, PASHA member list)
- First 2 audits at 50% off in exchange for testimonial + anonymized numbers + referral
- Join PASHA, TiE, NIC (National Incubation Center) cohorts — free clients, mentors, credibility
- Cold DM script: "Most exporters we talk to pay for 2–3 overlapping AI tools and can't answer
  client security questionnaires. 15-min call?"

### Days 46–90 — productize
- Build ledger-automation MVP on Supabase free tier (semester breaks)
- Fund domain/SECP/lawyer/SOC-2 prep from audit revenue
- Publish anonymized findings per audit as LinkedIn posts (content marketing)

## Pricing (student wedge)
- Free scan → lead
- 10-day audit: ₨150k–400k by company size (Big-4 charge ₨2M+ — that's the wedge)
- Certificate-only questionnaire pack: ₨50k–100k (recurring, low effort)
- Never % of savings (already on site — good)

## 6-month success metrics
5+ paid audits · ₨1M+ revenue · 3+ testimonials + 1 case study · 500+ free scans ·
1 enterprise client (50+ seats) · MVP live with 2 beta users

## Risks & mitigations
1. **Certificate trust** — unknown firm's certificate means little to US procurement. Mitigate: ISO 27001
   lead-auditor cert (cheap via NIC/PASHA workshops), Clutch/EU-Startups listings, recognizable logos fast.
2. **Time** — scope audits tightly; 10 days is the promise; reject scope creep.
3. **Liability** — engagement letter with liability cap; professional indemnity (cyber) insurance once
   revenue exists (~₨50–100k/yr PK); never sign unlimited-liability enterprise clauses.
