Privacy.

Who we are

Jaiza ("The Audit Duo") is an independent AI-spend assessment practice operating from Lahore and Karachi, Pakistan, serving clients remotely. Contact: contact@jaiza.com. Company registration details are available on request.

This website collects nothing

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers, no fingerprinting.
  • Every asset on this site — styles, scripts, fonts — is loaded from this site itself. We make no requests to any third-party domain (our fonts are self-hosted; our pages pass an automated CI check that fails if a third-party resource is ever referenced).
  • No forms submit anywhere. The contact button opens your own mail application.
  • Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages / Vercel) process routine technical request logs under their own privacy terms; we do not access or use them for tracking.

The free spend scan

The CSV file you drop or paste on the free scan page is parsed by your browser, on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and nothing is stored. Closing the tab erases it. The downloadable ledger preview is generated locally in your browser and saved by you, not by us.

Email

If you email us, we receive what you choose to send — including your address — and use it solely to reply and prepare an engagement. We do not add you to marketing lists, and we do not share your address.

Paid engagements

Client materials handled during a paid audit are governed by the signed engagement agreement, which includes confidentiality terms and — where required — a data processing agreement, documented retention periods, and secure destruction after the retention window. Our legal basis for processing client data is the performance of that contract.

Your rights

Write to contact@jaiza.com to ask what data we hold about you, or to request correction or deletion. We respond within 30 days.
