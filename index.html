WHAT THE AUDIT COVERS

Money and data, in one pass.

  1. 01

    Subscriptions & seats

    Utilization per seat and per team — who actually uses what, and which licenses are pure overhead.

  2. 02

    Shadow tools & vendor terms

    What actually leaves your network, to which services, and where sensitive data is at risk of exposure.
