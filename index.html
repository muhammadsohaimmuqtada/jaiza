THREE ENGAGEMENT PHASES

Wherever you are with AI, we start there.

Most companies are stuck in one of three places: not adopted yet, adopted but bleeding cost, or adopted without control. Jaiza covers all three — as a continuous path, not a one-off checklist.

AI adoption & mitigation

If your team has not fully moved to AI — or has done it chaotically — we map the business first, then design a transfer plan that fits your size, budget, data sensitivity, and technical capacity.

We do not push a single vendor. We decide, with you, whether the right path is an enterprise SaaS plan, API usage, a rented GPU/server running your own models, or hardware you own and operate locally.

  • Work & role audit Map departments, job types, and daily workflows so tooling matches how people actually work — not a generic seat count.
  • Tool & stack fit Recommend the right tier and architecture: ChatGPT/Claude enterprise, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, API-based builds, or self-hosted models.
  • Infrastructure options Evaluate local hardware, third-party GPU servers, private model hosting, and how each integrates into your existing systems.
  • Rollout & enablement Phased rollout by team, access rules, training, and integration into the tools you already use — so adoption sticks.

ROI & optimization audit

If you are already on AI, we pressure-test whether the plan and infrastructure are actually working in your favour. An AI bill that outruns the value it creates is not a transformation — it is a leak.

You get a clear report: what is paying off, what is idle, what should be scaled down, consolidated, or re-architected — including when the honest recommendation is to spend less.

  • Utilization review Which seats, APIs, and models are used — and which are paid for but idle.
  • Cost vs. value Tie spend to measurable outcomes: hours saved, throughput, revenue impact, error reduction.
  • Architecture tuning Right-size models and routes — expensive models only where they earn it; cheaper or local options everywhere else.
  • Profit-first recommendations Concrete changes to cut waste and increase return, with a before/after cost picture.

Governance & data control

Even a well-chosen enterprise plan fails if one employee pastes client data into a personal account. We audit how data actually moves, review vendor privacy terms, and put enforcement in front of the problem — not just a PDF policy on a shelf.

Where needed, we help install a control layer: traffic that tries to leave with critical data outside your approved enterprise path can be blocked or redirected.

  • Shadow AI discovery Find personal accounts, unmanaged tools, and gaps where approved access exists but is not used.
  • Vendor & privacy review Check retention, training use of data, and contractual terms on every AI service in play.
  • Policy that people follow Practical rules by role, with enforcement and periodic re-checks as tools and teams change.
  • Enforcement proxy Route AI traffic through controlled infrastructure. Critical data leaving outside the enterprise path can be dropped.
HOW ADOPTION WORKS

We transfer the structure — not just buy seats.

Moving a company onto AI means more than a subscription. We cover the full mitigation path: people, process, tooling, and infrastructure.

  1. 01

    Discover the real company

    Interviews, org structure, expense lines, and workflow walkthroughs. We document what each role does day to day — and every AI tool already in use, sanctioned or not.

  2. 02

    Design the mitigation plan

    Role-level recommendations: who needs what, at which tier, and why. Include change risks, training needs, and a phased sequence so the business keeps running while you migrate.

  3. 03

    Choose the right infrastructure

    Enterprise SaaS, API builds, rented GPU/server capacity, or owned hardware with models you control. Vendor-neutral — picked for budget, sensitivity, and your team’s ability to operate it.

  4. 04

    Integrate & enable

    Connect AI into your existing stack where it creates leverage, set access correctly, train teams, and confirm usage is real — not a license sitting unused.

  5. 05

    Measure, then lock down

    Confirm the plan is returning value. Then harden governance: policy, vendor terms, shadow-AI checks, and enforcement so the gains do not create new exposure.

THE CONTROL LAYER

Policy without enforcement is theatre.

Having an enterprise plan does not mean every employee is using it. One personal ChatGPT session with a client file is enough to break the promise you made to a foreign buyer — or to yourself.

Jaiza identifies those gaps and, where appropriate, helps you put a proxy in front of AI traffic: if something critical tries to leave your approved path, it does not leave.

  • Detect personal accounts and unmanaged tools
  • Review privacy & retention terms on every vendor
  • Block or redirect sensitive outbound AI traffic
  • Re-check as teams and tools change
