The exposure gap.

Employees are adopting AI tools to accelerate their work. Often, this happens through personal accounts, bypassing formal procurement and security review.

As a result, sensitive company documents, source code, and client data are routinely submitted to platforms with opaque retention policies. Most organizations remain unaware of this exposure until a client requests a security audit or issues a vendor questionnaire.

01 / SHADOW AI

Unmanaged adoption of consumer AI services within professional workflows.

02 / DATA EXPOSURE

Proprietary information processed by models without enterprise agreements.

03 / COMPLIANCE RISK

Inability to attest to secure data handling when requested by foreign partners.

OUR METHODOLOGY

A four-step governance audit.

01

Discovery

We analyze network traffic, expense reports, and employee workflows to document exactly which AI applications are actively used across your organization.

02

Risk Assessment

We evaluate the data policies of identified tools to determine where company intellectual property or client data may be exposed to third-party model training.

03

Optimization

We identify redundant software subscriptions, underutilized enterprise seats, and fragmented tooling to reduce your overall operational expenditure.

04

Fit Recommendation

We provide clear, vendor-neutral recommendations on the appropriate enterprise-grade tools that balance productivity requirements with strict data security.

THE OUTCOME

A report you can act on, and a certificate you can show clients.

At the conclusion of the audit, you receive a comprehensive document detailing current exposures, financial inefficiencies, and an actionable remediation plan.

Alongside the internal report, you receive a formal certificate of review. This serves as objective documentation that your organization has evaluated its AI supply chain—an increasingly mandatory requirement for companies serving US, UK, and Gulf-based clients.

SAMPLE REF: JZ-2024-8842-A

The requirement is already here.

Foreign enterprises are systematically updating their vendor security questionnaires to include strict AI data handling policies. A growing number of clients now require explicit attestation regarding how their data interacts with external language models.

Many organizations lack a definitive answer. An audit provides the baseline necessary to establish policy, secure data, and maintain client trust.
