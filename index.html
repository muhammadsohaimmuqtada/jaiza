Where most companies stand today.

Employees are already using AI tools to get their work done faster. Often this happens through personal accounts, without any policy, review, or plan behind it — not because anyone is being careless, but because no one has given them a safer alternative yet.

The result is twofold. Sensitive company data — documents, source code, client information — ends up inside tools with no enterprise agreement and no clear retention policy. And at the same time, the company gets none of the benefit of a properly adopted AI strategy: no consistent tooling, no training, no measurable productivity gain, just scattered, ungoverned use.

Most organizations don't notice either problem until a client asks a direct question about it.

01 / SHADOW AI

Unmanaged, ungoverned AI use across the organization — with no visibility into what's actually happening.

02 / DATA EXPOSURE

Company and client data processed by tools with no enterprise agreement or clear retention policy.

03 / STALLED ADOPTION

AI used inconsistently or informally, without the training or governance needed to turn it into a real advantage.

OUR METHODOLOGY

How we work.

01

Discovery

We map exactly which AI tools are already in use across your organization — sanctioned or not — through employee interviews, expense records, and workflow review.

02

Risk Assessment

We evaluate the data-handling policy of every tool in use, identifying where company or client data may be exposed to third-party model training or unclear retention terms.

03

Optimization & Governance Plan

We remove redundant subscriptions, right-size your licensing, and build a governance policy your team can actually follow — so safe AI use becomes the default, not an afterthought.

04

Implementation & Rollout

We don't just recommend a plan — we help you put it into practice: rolling out approved tools, training your team, and making sure adoption sticks instead of quietly reverting to personal accounts.

ONGOING GOVERNANCE

AI governance isn't a one-time fix.

New tools appear constantly, teams grow, and usage habits drift back to old patterns without reinforcement. A single audit gives you a starting point — staying protected means checking in as your organization changes.

We offer periodic governance reviews to confirm your policies are still being followed, your tooling still fits your needs, and no new data-exposure risk has quietly appeared since your last audit — so your certificate reflects reality, not a snapshot from a year ago.

THE OUTCOME

A report you can act on, and a certificate you can show clients.

At the conclusion of the audit, you receive a clear report: what's currently in use, where the risks are, what it's costing you, and a practical plan to fix it — plus hands-on support putting that plan into action.

Alongside the report, you receive a formal certificate of review — objective documentation that your organization has evaluated and governed its AI usage. For companies serving clients in the US, UK, or Gulf, this is fast becoming a standard requirement, not an optional extra.

The requirement is already here.
