# Jaiza — Outreach Playbook (cold DM + email)

Goal: **10 conversations → 2–3 paid audits** in the first 45 days (first 2 at 50% off in
exchange for a testimonial + anonymized numbers + referral). No paid tools — LinkedIn free
tier + the `?ref=` tag on the site tells us where leads came from.

---

## Who to target (in order of hit-rate)

1. **Software exporters, 40–300 staff, Lahore/Karachi/Islamabad** with US/UK/Gulf clients —
   they get vendor security questionnaires and their CFO sees a growing AI line item.
2. **BPO & IT-services firms** serving US healthcare/finance — questionnaires are brutal there.
3. **Agencies that just raised or landed an enterprise logo** — new procurement scrutiny.

Where to find them (all free):
- PSEB registered-companies directory · PASHA member list · LinkedIn search
  (`"software house" Lahore` → filter 2nd-degree, post about their US clients)
- LinkedIn posts mentioning "SOC 2", "vendor questionnaire", "client security review"
- Your own university alumni network (warmest channel — start there)

## The ref-tag convention

Every link you send carries a source tag so the contact form tells us where they came from:

`https://jaiza-chi.vercel.app/?ref=li-dm-<first-name>` · `?ref=pasha` · `?ref=alumni` ·
`?ref=scan-post` (for the free-scan posts). Scan page takes it too: `/scan.html?ref=li-dm-ali`.

## Cold DM script — LinkedIn (founder / CFO / CTO)

**Opening message (connection request, no pitch — 300 char limit):**

> Salaam [Name] — I work with Pakistani software exporters on AI tooling costs and client
> security questionnaires. Watching [Company]'s US work from afar and would like to connect.

**After they accept (the actual DM — send within 24h of accept):**

> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Quick question — [Company] ships for US clients, so at some
> point a client's procurement team sends the AI/security questionnaire. Most exporters we
> talk to are paying for 2–3 overlapping AI tools at the same time *and* can't answer that
> questionnaire cleanly.
>
> We run a fixed-fee 10-day audit that ends in two documents: a spend ledger your CFO acts
> on, and a signed certificate of review that answers the client questionnaire in one
> attachment. No % of savings, one quoted number.
>
> Worth 15 minutes? If it's easier to see first — a real sample of both documents is here:
> https://jaiza-chi.vercel.app/#deliverables
>
> And if you're just curious what your AI spend looks like, the free scan parses a card
> statement entirely in your browser (nothing uploads): https://jaiza-chi.vercel.app/scan.html

**Rules:**
- Never send the pitch unprompted with the connection request — acceptance rate collapses.
- One follow-up after 4 days, then stop. Follow-up: "No worries if the timing's off,
  [Name] — the free scan stays up. Good luck with the [client/milestone] push."
- Personalize ONE thing per DM (their client vertical, a recent post, a PSEB listing).
- 20 DMs/week, tracked in a simple sheet: name · company · date · ref-tag · reply · outcome.
