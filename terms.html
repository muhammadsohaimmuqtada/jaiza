What we do is an independent spend assessment. It is not a statutory audit — under the Companies Act 2017 only chartered accountants may audit financial statements — and it is not a legal certification, attestation, legal advice, tax advice, or financial advice.

1. The free scan

The free spend scan is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. It produces estimates from the data you supply, entirely in your browser; it does not reconcile against invoices, admin consoles, or network traffic. Only the paid audit does that.

2. Paid engagements

Paid audits are governed by a signed engagement letter, which fixes scope, duration, fee, and deliverables, and includes confidentiality terms and a limitation of liability. Where required, a data processing agreement governs client data. If any signed engagement conflicts with these terms, the engagement letter prevails for that engagement.

3. Your responsibilities as a client

  • Provide accurate records and the authority to share them.
  • Redact full card numbers before sharing statements — dates, merchants, and amounts are sufficient.
  • Grant access only to the systems listed in the engagement's authorization schedule, on a read-only basis.

4. The certificate of review

The certificate is a point-in-time independent review document. Its value and limits are stated on its face: it attests only to the scope, period, and method described in it, for the company named in it. It is not a certification of ongoing compliance and must not be represented as one.
